# Story styles

Pick ONE key. Each style gives the antagonist type, the hero's personality and the finale payoff. `custom` follows the user's own plot.

## `neighbor` — เพื่อนบ้าน/ร้านข้างๆ อวดดี

**Finale payoff:** หมายศาล

The antagonist is a neighbour or rival shop owner: arrogant, not very smart, argues in circles, twists facts, plays the victim, claims to act 'for everyone' and never admits fault. The hero is calm and keeps evidence (CCTV, chats, receipts). Side characters get hurt by the antagonist's antics and slowly turn against them. Finale: a court summons (หมายศาล) finally makes the antagonist back down and apologise.

## `family` — ครอบครัว/ญาติเอาเปรียบ

**Finale payoff:** แยกทางแล้วรุ่ง

Family or relatives exploit the hero. Example beat: the elder brother's fishing boat made 6 million baht only because the hero ran everything, yet he hands the hero a 600 baht bonus and calls it generous. Parents and old relatives always side with the favoured child and never listen to the hero to the end; they cut him off mid-sentence. The hero quietly leaves and starts his own business (e.g. his own fishing boat), and because all the real know-how was his, he thrives while the family business collapses. Relatives come back to beg, demand a share, guilt-trip ('we are family'), spread rumours, and get calmly turned down. Write plenty of sharp direct dialogue between family members; the audience must feel the injustice and then the satisfaction.

## `system` — ระบบพิเศษ (โดนไล่แล้วได้ระบบ)

**Finale payoff:** ได้ระบบแล้วพลิกชีวิต

Modern-day Thailand 'system' story (like Chinese 系统流 web dramas). The hero is humiliated and kicked out (fired, evicted or thrown out by family). At his lowest point a mysterious SYSTEM activates that only he can hear. The system speaks in short bracketed notices, e.g. [ระบบ: ตรวจพบภารกิจใหม่ — ...], [ภารกิจ: ...], [รางวัล: ...], [ผสานไอเท็ม: เชือก + ตะขอ = ...], [ตรวจสอบ: ...]. It gives main quests, hidden quests with rare rewards, time limits and penalties, item fusion ('ผสาน'), and instant expert knowledge (scanning ingredients, antiques, engines, prices). Rewards must be concrete and useful (a skill, a rare item, knowledge, money) and each quest turns into a face-slapping win over the people who looked down on him. Keep the system a bit cheeky. The hero keeps the system secret; others think he just got lucky or smart.

## `food` — ธุรกิจอาหาร/เชฟถูกโกง

**Finale payoff:** ฝีมือชนะ

Food business revenge. The hero is a brilliant cook who truly knows ingredients (freshness, cuts, sourcing, seasoning). His brother or partner made their restaurant number one thanks to him, then refuses the agreed dividend and throws him out. The hero opens his own small place and wins customers with real skill. The antagonist sabotages: sends people to make scenes, fake reviews, slander, price cutting, health-inspector tip-offs, while secretly using 10-year-old frozen meat and loads of seasoning powder. Each episode the hero wins with craft and real food knowledge explained in one short, true, interesting sentence the audience learns from, and the antagonist's shortcuts are exposed in public.

## `rider` — ไรเดอร์ถูกดูถูก

**Finale payoff:** ตัวจริงเปิดเผย

The hero is a delivery rider who is quiet, polite and says little. Customers, a restaurant manager, a rich in-law or an ex look down on him, make him wait, blame him, refuse to pay, post him online. He stays calm and keeps proof (order history, GPS, dashcam). He has a hidden side (a real skill, a past achievement, or a hidden identity that is revealed gradually, never all at once). Every episode ends with a small reveal that makes the people who mocked him look foolish; the big reveal is saved for the finale.

## `office` — ออฟฟิศ/หัวหน้าขโมยผลงาน

**Finale payoff:** ตัวร้ายโดนไล่ออก พระเอกได้เลื่อนขั้น

Malicious compliance at a Thai office. The antagonist is a smooth-talking boss or brown-nosing coworker who steals credit and quietly shifts blame; polite to your face, stabs you in the back, never shouts. The hero is very capable but plays dumb: he follows the boss's wrong orders exactly, 100%, and screenshots every LINE instruction. He deliberately steps back so the villain walks into a trap of his own making. Example beat: the boss grabs the big client project, edits the numbers wrongly and orders on LINE 'don't touch it, do it my way'; on the board meeting day it collapses in front of the directors and the hero shows the chat.

## `ex` — แฟนเก่า/คนรักนอกใจ

**Finale payoff:** คู่รักจอมปลอมแตกคอกันกลางงาน

Cheating ex and fake lover, cunning payback. The antagonists are an ex who two-timed or left for money and the new show-off partner; they cry on cue, play the victim and post snide stories on social media. The hero is not a deadpan robot: he is playful and sly, pretends to be pitiful so the villains relax and reveal their true colours themselves. Example beat: the ex leaves him to get engaged to a 'rich tycoon' and invites him to the engagement to mock him; he arrives with a gift envelope, then on stage hands over a court summons for the tycoon's unpaid rental car and fake watch.

## `craft` — ช่างซ่อม/อาชีพเฉพาะทาง

**Finale payoff:** ตัวร้ายเสียเงินมากกว่าเดิมสิบเท่าและต้องขอโทษ

Skilled tradesman underpaid. The antagonist is a stingy customer or greedy contractor who mocks the hero's fee: 'it's a five-minute job, why so expensive'. The hero is an honest, skilled tradesman (air-con, electrician, mechanic, IT) with ethics who refuses to be exploited. He wins through time and technical results: the villain breaks things himself by hiring cheap or doing it wrong. Example beat: a luxury car owner refuses to pay 3,500 baht for a key repair, calls the hero a crook and goes to a backyard garage; three days later the engine dies on the expressway during his live stream and he begs the hero, whose fee is now 50,000 baht. Explain one true, interesting technical fact per episode.

## `rewind` — ย้อนเวลากลับมาแก้เกม

**Finale payoff:** ตัวร้ายติดหนี้ท่วมหัว พระเอกรวยเงียบ

Second chance, back in time. In his past life the hero was betrayed by a best friend or partner who made him co-sign a loan or stole his assets. He wakes up 3-5 years before it all happened and knows what is coming (which land a new road or train line will cross, the friend's scam). The hero is on the offence from the start, quietly setting traps instead of waiting to be bullied; he narrates what happened 'last time'. Example beat: the day before the friend tricks him into a joint loan, he cheerfully encourages the friend to pledge his own house instead, while he secretly buys cheap landlocked plots that a train line will cross in six months.

## `condo` — ปะทะขาใหญ่/นิติคอนโด

**Finale payoff:** เส้นสายช่วยไม่ได้ แพ้กฎระเบียบและค่าปรับรายวัน

Entitled big shot versus the rules. The antagonist is a privileged neighbour who brags about knowing 'big people' and the police, parks blocking others, has noisy dogs or builds onto common areas, and threatens staff. The hero is an ordinary, easily irritated guy with flaws, but he knows the law, the condo or village bylaws and every official complaint channel (Traffy Fondue, the consumer protection board, the district office) by heart. Example beat: a tycoon parks his supercar across the EV charging bay every day and threatens to get the guard fired; the hero films it, sends it to the juristic office, digs up two years of unpaid common fees and gets his key card cut and his wheel clamped by a meeting resolution.

## `scam` — กระชากหน้ากากสายมูปลอม/ไลฟ์โค้ช

**Finale payoff:** เพจแฉแชร์ต่อ ตำรวจลงพื้นที่ ตัวร้ายปิดเพจหนี

Exposing a fake guru. The antagonist is a lottery-tip master, a medium or a get-rich coach who sounds credible, has paid fans cheering, and fleeces old people including the hero's family. Calm and smiling, not loud. The hero is an observant online sleuth who goes undercover as a disciple to collect the backstage truth. Instead of a two-person argument, he wakes up the whole group of victims at once. Example beat: his mother is about to transfer 300,000 baht for 'healing holy water' from a famous TikTok master; he sends the water to a lab, checks that the luxury car is rented by the day, then asks the question live in front of tens of thousands of viewers.

## `swap` — สลับฐานะ/ตัวปลอมแอบอ้าง

**Finale payoff:** ตัวร้ายหน้าชาจนต้องมุดโต๊ะหนีออกจากงาน

Hidden boss, comedy of a fake big shot. Lighter and funnier than the other styles: laugh-out-loud satisfying, not heavy drama. The antagonist is a show-off who fakes wealth and name-drops important people to look down on others. The hero is the real deal dressed plainly (T-shirt, shorts, old car), playful, and plays along innocently to watch the villain dig himself deeper. Example beat: at a class reunion a classmate brags that he is close to 'Sia Ek', owner of the hotel hosting the party, and sends the hero to the last table for his market T-shirt; the hero innocently asks 'what does Sia Ek look like?' until the hotel manager walks in with a cake and bows to the hero.

## `custom` — พล็อตของเราเอง (AI แต่งต่อ)

Follow the user's plot idea closely and expand it into a full face-slapping drama in the same spirit.
